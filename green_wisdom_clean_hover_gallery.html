Green Wisdom (Suitable for ages 60 and up!)

Understanding our environment and what we can do to care for it, together.

Our programmes bring seniors together through hands-on upcycling, practical skills and guided outdoor activities. Participants can learn something new, share their experiences and create useful items for their homes and community spaces.

Venue: In your centre or one of our partner venues.
Located near:
  • Tai Seng
  • Botanic Gardens
  • Maxwell

Outdoor walks take place at suitable nature sites.

Fees:
  • Green Wisdom - S$65 per workshop
  • Bring a friend - S$110 per workshop for two
