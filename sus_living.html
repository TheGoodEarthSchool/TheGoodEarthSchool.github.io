Sustainable Living Workshops (Suitable for all ages*)

*Kids under 10 must be accompanied by an adult

Small choices can make a difference. Our Sustainable Living Workshops help participants compare the materials and products used in everyday life, understand their environmental impacts, and consider more sustainable alternatives to conventional options. Through hands-on activities and guided discussions, participants learn to look beyond product labels and make informed choices based on ingredients, sourcing, durability, reuse and disposal.

Workshops in this series

  • Jesmonite Workshop
  • Clay x Food Waste Craft
  • Diatomite Workshop
  • Eco-enzymes
  • Natural Dye Making and Craft
  • Making Your Own Beer / Fruit Wine
  • Cork Book Binding
  • Art Jam Using Upcycled / Recycled Materials
  • Soy Wax Lip Balm (with Cooking Oil)
  • Or have something else in mind (that meets our focus areas)? ↓
