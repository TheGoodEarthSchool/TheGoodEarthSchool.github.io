Volunteer With Us

Suitable for ages 15 and up!

We always hope to have more individuals join us, to do good for the animals, the environment, and the communities we serve.

What you can do:

  • Volunteer Facilitator & Befriender for Inclusive Guided Nature Walks
  • Volunteer Facilitator for Beach / Urban Cleanups
  • Volunteer Educator for Recycled Furniture Making
  • Volunteer Educator on Environmental Sustainability, Ecology, and Care
  • Or have something else in mind?
Talk to us!

Things to note:

  • Volunteers aged 15 years must be accompanied by a parent or guardian.
  • Volunteers below 21 years of age require parental or guardian consent.
  • Volunteers should be able to communicate respectfully with participants, fellow volunteers, and staff.
  • Volunteers are expected to follow programme guidelines, safety procedures, and staff instructions at all times.
  • Please arrive punctually for your confirmed volunteer session.
  • Some volunteer opportunities may involve interactions with animals. Training and guidance will be provided, and volunteers are expected to follow all animal welfare and handling protocols.
  • Some programmes may take place in parks, gardens, forests, farms, beaches, or other natural environments. Volunteers should be prepared for outdoor conditions and wear appropriate attire.
  • Closed-toe shoes, comfortable clothing, and a reusable water bottle are recommended unless otherwise stated.

Seniors Doing Good

Doing good has no age limit. Seniors bring valuable knowledge, experiences and skills that can be shared with others through meaningful community engagements.

If you are an Active Ageing Centre and want to do good together to engage the community, we would love to have a chat.

Programme Image Seniors Doing Good

Seniors Take Charge

Initially catered for seniors, by seniors, we started to realise, why limit it to fellow seniors? It has now become a programme by seniors, for everyone.

We focus on these key areas:
  • Activities that encourage occupational function.
  • Engagements that enhance quality of life through green activities.
  • Reconnecting with animals and the environment, together.
Activities include, but are not limited to:
  • Skincare classes using food waste.
  • Nature walks together!
  • Making furniture together, curated and taught by seniors, for everyone.

Doing Good for the Community

There are many ways to contribute to the places and people around us. From shared activities to caring for community spaces, we hope to bring people together to do something meaningful.

For now, our emphasis is on building up community spaces and encouraging creative expression through murals and other art forms.

Programme Image Doing Good for the Community

Making a Difference Together

Participants work together across community groups to do good for the community, from setting up communal spaces in our shared areas to expressing the kampung spirit through murals and other art forms.

We focus on these key areas:
  • Furniture upcycling to set up spaces for everyone using rescued or recycled items.
  • Expression through nature-related murals and community art.
  • Bonding across community groups.
Activities include:
  • Mural art workshops across community groups.
  • Setting up communal spaces in shared areas.
  • Furniture rescue, upcycling and refreshing workshops.
