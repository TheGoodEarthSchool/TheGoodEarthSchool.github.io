Parenting Talks - Nurturing Kindness to Animals at Home

What will be covered:

  1. Simple yet meaningful ways to foster compassion and responsibility for animals.
  2. Fun, hands-on ideas for building bonds with pets or connecting with nature.
  3. TGES resources to support you.

Who should attend?

Parents eager to teach their children how to care for animals and respect all living beings.

Parenting Talks - Nurturing Environmental Stewardship at Home

What will be covered:

  1. How to instil environmental stewardship at home.
  2. Simple eco-friendly practices for busy families.
  3. TGES resources to support you.

Why it matters:

Teaching environmental stewardship at home equips children with the mindset and habits to live sustainably.

Who should attend?

Parents eager to cultivate sustainability values in their children.

Career Pathways

Topics covered:

  • Why we need the sustainability industry.
  • The relationship between the industry and day-to-day consumers.
  • Understanding the sustainability industry and possible career paths.

Why it matters:

Understanding the sustainability sector helps participants connect their interests and skills to possible career options.

Who should attend?

Individuals who want to find out more about jobs in the sustainability sector, including fresh graduates, IHL interns and career switchers.

Corporate Talks

Topics covered:

  • The case for sustainability.
  • Current circular economy landscape.
  • What each individual can do.

Who should attend?

Corporates and departments interested in reducing waste, their carbon footprint, or simply learning how to live more sustainably.

Meet The Speakers

Dr Cynthia Wong

About the speaker

Dr Cynthia Wong is a seasoned marine ecologist with 10 years of experience in the academic and research industry.

She is committed to helping young individuals develop a deep understanding and appreciation for marine ecology.

Her dedication has motivated her to educate young minds at different Institutes of Higher Education. Through her teaching, she not only imparts knowledge but also cultivates close collaboration with academia and conservation partners, enhancing her students' learning experiences in science-related fields.

Coach Aifa Ahmad

About the speaker

Aifa Ahmad is a seasoned trainer and coach with over 20 years of experience in teambuilding, facilitation, and outdoor education.

Her passion for training began when she was a teen, where she saw the value of sharing knowledge and inspiring others through outdoor training. Over the years, Aifa has cultivated expertise in wellness and entrepreneurship, delivering programmes that support individuals and foster personal growth.

Her work has reached diverse audiences, from students to professionals, as she continues to champion meaningful connections and positive change through her facilitation.

Jeryl Yep

About the speaker

Jeryl Yep is an innovator and trained chemical engineer with a wealth of experience in environmental leadership. His career path began with roles focused on driving environmental responsibility, including his time as an environmental engineer at a local NGO, where he led certifications, product development, and training programmes.

He also championed energy-saving initiatives and guided students and industries in adopting more sustainable practices. Jeryl continues to inspire collaboration and create solutions that support individuals and businesses in making a lasting impact on the environment.
