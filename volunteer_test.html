01. Volunteer With Us

Suitable for ages 15 and up!

We always hope to have more individuals join us, to do good for the animals, the environment, and the communities we serve.

What you can do:

  • Volunteer Facilitator & Befriender for Inclusive Guided Nature Walks
  • Volunteer Facilitator for Beach / Urban Cleanups
  • Volunteer Educator for Recycled Furniture Making
  • Volunteer Educator on Environmental Sustainability, Ecology, and Care
  • Or have something else in mind? ↓
Talk to us!

Things to note:
  • Volunteers aged 15 years must be accompanied by a parent or guardian.
  • Volunteers below 21 years of age require parental or guardian consent.
  • Volunteers should be able to communicate respectfully with participants, fellow volunteers, and staff.
  • Volunteers are expected to follow programme guidelines, safety procedures, and staff instructions at all times.
  • Please arrive punctually for your confirmed volunteer session.
  • Some volunteer opportunities may involve interactions with animals. Training and guidance will be provided, and volunteers are expected to follow all animal welfare and handling protocols.
  • Some programmes may take place in parks, gardens, forests, farms, beaches, or other natural environments. Volunteers should be prepared for outdoor conditions and wear appropriate attire.
  • Closed-toe shoes, comfortable clothing, and a reusable water bottle are recommended unless otherwise stated.
